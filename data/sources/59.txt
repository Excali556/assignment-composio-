Waterfall API introduction

Copy pageCopy page

Explore Waterfall’s APIs and compare their inputs, results, and use cases to choose the right endpoint for your workflow.

Copy pageCopy page

The Waterfall API is available at https://api.waterfall.io. Send your API key in the x-api-key header. See Authentication.
Endpoints

API

Type

Description

Prospector

Async

Find people at a company by domain and title filters, with available emails and optional phones.

Search Contact

Sync

Find profiles by LinkedIn, company, or supported company-set and role filters. Emails and phones are excluded.

Search Company

Sync

Find companies by industry, headquarters country, or headcount range. Preview.

Contact Enrichment

Async

Find profile data and available contact details from an email, LinkedIn identifier, or name and company domain.

Phone Enrichment

Async

Find available phone numbers for a known person.

Company Enrichment

Async

Find company details from a domain, LinkedIn identifier, or company name.

Company Reveal

Sync

Look up company context from an IPv4 address, with entity type and confidence.

Company Titles

Sync

Retrieve active job titles at a company by domain or LinkedIn identifier. Preview; requires access on your key.

Job Change

Sync

Check a contact’s company information and return an employment-change outcome with an available profile.

Email Verification

Sync

Check a specific email address and return its verification status.

Webhook JWKS

Sync

Fetch public Ed25519 keys for verifying signed webhook deliveries.

Account Reporting

Sync

Read usage for the account and authenticated key, plus the current account balance.

API Key Management

Sync

List account keys and create or update sub-keys using a master key.

Choose an API based on what you already know and what you want to find. The guidance below separates search, enrichment, verification, and employment checks.

Search Contact or Prospector? Use Search Contact when you need profile search and structured filters. Use Prospector when you have a target account and want to find people and contact details by title. To enrich someone found through Search Contact, pass their LinkedIn identifier to Contact Enrichment. See account prospecting for the workflow.

Check a contact’s employment
Job Change checks a contact’s company information and returns a status with an available profile snapshot. Start with a supported company-and-contact identifier combination or a professional email. If you need recurring checks, your workflow schedules the requests and compares the results. See job changes.
When do results arrive?
For async APIs, the launcher returns a job_id. Retrieve the result with the matching Finder or receive it through webhook_url.For sync data APIs, read the result in the POST response. Where a Finder exists, use it to retrieve the saved result later. Email Verification has no Finder.The job’s status describes processing. Inspect the returned data to decide whether it is useful for your task. A SUCCEEDED job does not promise that every requested field was found.

Search Company and Company Titles are Preview APIs. Their behavior may change. Check the relevant reference before building a critical workflow around a Preview API.
