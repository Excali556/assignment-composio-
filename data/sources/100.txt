Frequently Asked Questions

What is Grain?

Grain is an AI notetaker and conversation intelligence platform that records video calls, Slack huddles, and in-person meetings to provide accurate summaries, insights, and coaching feedback. Unlike other notetaking tools, Grain captures all meeting content: video, desktop captured audio, and resulting transcripts and notes. We make it easy to share meaningful data with your clients and collaborators, and to transform your meeting data into actionable insights and deliverables using AI technology.

Who is Grain for?

Grain serves any team that relies on meetings, but we specialize in small to mid-market and enterprise sales, customer success, and product teams. These teams benefit most from automated meeting capture, call analysis, team coaching features, and AI-powered deliverables using meeting data that save time and improve collaboration.

How do I use Grain with my team?

Send clips, share notes, and deliver feedback to teammates with Grain's built-in Teams feature. Create groups to organize and automatically share specific meeting types with exactly who needs access, streamlining collaboration.

‍

How much does Grain cost?

Grain is free forever for individuals. Pro is $15/month for one person. Business is $29 per seat/month, billed annually (2-seat minimum), and adds a shared workspace for your team.

What's included in the free plan?

Unlimited meetings up to 45 minutes each, 30-day history, unlimited AI notes, live transcripts, bot-free capture, and Claude & ChatGPT connections.

How much time do I save with Grain vs. manual note-taking?

On average, users save about 10 minutes per meeting by eliminating manual note-taking and follow-ups. Over a month, that's 20+ hours per team member that can be reinvested in coaching, strategy, or revenue-driving activities instead.

Is Grain secure and compliant?

Yes. Grain holds SOC 2 Type II. All data is hosted on AWS infrastructure in the USA within a dedicated virtual private cloud (VPC). Grain conducts annual penetration testing by third-party firms (Prescient Security) and maintains an A+ Qualys/SSL Labs rating. User authentication uses Google, Microsoft, SSO, or SAML. For more details, see https://grain.com/security.

Do I need permission to record meetings with Grain?

In most regions you are required to inform participants that a meeting is being recorded, and in some jurisdictions you must obtain explicit consent from all parties. Grain helps by clearly indicating when a bot is present or recording, but you remain responsible for complying with local recording and privacy laws.

Does Grain share my data with AI providers?

Grain uses AI models to provide its services. Neither Grain nor its subprocessors use your data for model training. See a list of our subprocessors here: https://grain.com/grain-subprocessors.

‍

Can I delete my meetings and data from Grain?

Yes. You can delete individual meetings, entire sets of recordings, or your workspace data from within Grain. When you delete content, it is removed from your workspace and associated sharing targets, and Grain enforces backend deletion according to its data retention and security policies.

How do CRM integrations work?

Grain integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and other leading CRMs to automatically sync meeting summaries, transcripts, and insights to deal records. See how each integration works at https://grain.com/integrations.

How does Grain compare to competitors like Fathom, Gong, or tl;dv?

Grain offers simple, transparent pricing starting at $15/month with no hidden fees or contracts. Unlike enterprise-heavy alternatives, Grain delivers powerful conversation intelligence at a fraction of typical costs while maintaining ease of use and fast deployment.

Why should we transcribe our meetings?

Transcripts create a searchable record of work completed in meetings, which AI then converts into actionable summaries. Without transcripts, meeting outcomes remain invisible to the organization, creating inefficiencies in follow-up and institutional knowledge sharing.

What languages does Grain support?

Grain supports over 130 languages for meeting transcription and AI notes. Common languages like English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, and Japanese deliver the highest accuracy with speaker recognition and punctuation. Over 100 additional languages including Arabic, Hebrew, and Thai are also supported.
